Section 7 · Lesson 44

Maximal Marginal Relevance (MMR)

Similarity search returns the closest documents even when they all say the same thing. MMR returns documents that are relevant and different from each other (the "mmr" search type mentioned in Lesson 33).

The problem: redundant results

"LangChain is good for generative AI applications" and "LangChain is good for generative AI and more complex applications" are both relevant, but nearly identical: the second wastes a slot. "LangChain is also used to build agentic AI applications" adds something new. MMR prefers that mix.

Definition

MMR is a diversity-aware retrieval technique that balances relevance (close to the query) and novelty (not redundant with what's already picked).

The formula

MMR(d) = λ × sim(d, q) − (1 − λ) × max sim(d, s)
                                     s ∈ S
  • λ × sim(d, q): relevance of candidate d to the query q.
  • (1 − λ) × max sim(d, s): similarity to the closest already-selected document s. It's subtracted, so near-duplicates are penalised.
  • λ: the balance. 0.7 = 70% relevance, 30% diversity; λ = 1 is plain similarity search.

Worked example

Query: "How to use LangChain for RAG?" D1 "LangChain enables retrieval with FAISS", D2 "LangChain can use Chroma and Pinecone too", D3 "LangChain agents can call external tools".

Similarity to the query

D1 0.95, D2 0.93, D3 0.80

Similarity between documents

D1–D2 0.90 (both about vector stores), D1–D3 0.30, D2–D3 0.40

Step 1: take the most relevant: D1.

Step 2: score the rest against D1, with λ = 0.7:

CandidateRelevancePenaltyMMR
D20.7 × 0.93 = 0.6510.3 × 0.90 = 0.270.381
D30.7 × 0.80 = 0.560.3 × 0.30 = 0.090.47

D2 is more relevant but nearly a copy of D1, so D3 is picked. (D2–D3 would only matter at a third pick.)

Similarity search would return D1 and D2, two sentences about vector stores. MMR returns D1 and D3, covering more ground.

Takeaways

  • MMR = relevance minus redundancy, weighted by λ.
  • Pick the most relevant first, then each time the candidate with the highest MMR against everything picked so far.

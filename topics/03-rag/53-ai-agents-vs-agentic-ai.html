Section 10 · Lesson 53

AI Agents vs Agentic AI

Two terms used for the rest of the course. An AI agent is one program doing one job. Agentic AI is a system of many agents working together on a bigger goal.

Definitions

AI agent

An individual program that performs a specific task with some autonomy (no human needed for that task), following predefined rules.

Agentic AI

A system where multiple agents collaborate and make decisions independently towards a larger goal, adapting to real-time data and learning from experience.

(The background entry describes an agent more loosely, as an LLM that picks its own next step; the course uses "agentic AI" for multi-agent systems.)

Key differences

AI agentAgentic AI
ScopeOne specific task, limited autonomyThe whole system; learns, adapts, makes complex decisions
DecisionsPredefined rulesAutonomous, based on real-time data
In shortA single entityA network of agents with a shared goal

Examples

ExampleTypeWhy
Customer service chatbot: "When is my order arriving?" → looks it up in the company database → answersAI agentOne function. In LLM terms: an LLM plus a database acting as a retriever, the same idea as RAG.
Banking bots: balances, transactions, opening accountsAI agentClearly defined processes, one agent per task.
Smart home: agents for lights, thermostat and appliances, with the goal of saving electricityAgentic AIThey coordinate based on the environment, e.g. lights off when nobody's in the room.
Personalised health assistant: medical history, live health data, lifestyleAgentic AIGoal-driven, adapts to new research and patient feedback.

An agentic system's decisions follow four steps:

  1. PerceiveGather data from the surroundings.
  2. ReasonWork out what's going on.
  3. ActE.g. switch off the lights.
  4. LearnImprove over time (e.g. reinforcement learning).

In the agents the course builds with LangGraph, an LLM is the brain: it decides what to do, and uses tools and databases to do it.

Takeaways

  • AI agent: one task, predefined rules.
  • Agentic AI: many agents, one goal, adaptive decisions (perceive → reason → act → learn).

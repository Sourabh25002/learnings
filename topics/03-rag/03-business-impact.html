Section 1 · Lesson 3

RAG in the Real World

Where RAG is used and why businesses choose it: a customer support example, and the results companies have reported.

Example: customer support

A customer asks:

What's your return policy for items bought during the Black Friday sale?

Without RAG

The LLM has none of this company's data, so it answers generically:

Generally, most companies offer 30-day
returns, but policies may vary.

Generic and unhelpful.

With RAG

The company's policies are in a vector database; RAG finds the right one and the LLM summarises it:

According to our current policy document
(version 3.2, uploaded November 2024),
Black Friday purchases have an extended
60-day return window until the 31st…

Specific, accurate, and it cites its source.

Two things make this work: the answer comes from the current policy document (policies change, here to version 3.2), and the vector database holds the company's own data.

Reported results

Case studies the instructor collected (figures as presented in the course):

CompanyResult
JP MorganResearch analysts moved from fine-tuning (~$200M a year) to RAG (~$50M): $150M saved a year.
Microsoft CopilotHallucination rate fell from 34% to 2% after adding RAG: a 94% reduction.
BloombergIts financial assistant gets new market data every hour. A traditional LLM needs a ~6-month training cycle to learn new things; RAG updates in real time (~24× faster).
HealthcareAnswers always cite approved medical sources, for compliance.

Why businesses choose RAG

  • Cheaper than fine-tuning (retraining the model on your own data, explained next lesson).
  • Far fewer hallucinations, because answers are grounded in documents.
  • Always current: add new data to the database; no retraining.
  • Compliance: source attribution (every answer points to where it came from), a complete audit trail, and answers only from approved sources.

Takeaways

  • RAG turns generic answers into specific, sourced ones from a company's own, current data.
  • Reported wins: big cost savings vs fine-tuning, far fewer hallucinations, real-time updates, compliance.
